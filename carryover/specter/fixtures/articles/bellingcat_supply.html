This investigation traces a single shipment of dual-use machine tools

from Hong Kong to a workshop on the outskirts of Yekaterinburg. The

shipment was flagged by Western export-control authorities in late

2023 after a containerised shipping manifest, reviewed by Bellingcat,

listed the cargo as "industrial spare parts" — a category historically

used to disguise high-precision items subject to sanctions.

Open-source records show that the consignee, a company registered in

Sverdlovsk Oblast, is one of three known front companies linked to a

sanctioned Russian defence procurement network. The same network has

been the subject of prior reporting in this series. The shipment

arrived in Yekaterinburg in February 2024 and was offloaded at a

private logistics yard inside an industrial park that also houses

several small machine shops.

The investigation combines export data, satellite imagery from

Planet's PlanetScope constellation, and corporate filings from at

least four jurisdictions. We were able to confirm the receipt by

matching containers visible in PlanetScope imagery from 12 February

2024 against the unique markings logged in the Hong Kong shipping

manifest.

A reporter for Bellingcat reached out to the registered director of

the consignee company; calls and emails received no response. The

relevant export-control authorities declined to comment on the

specific shipment but confirmed that investigations into similar

patterns are ongoing.
